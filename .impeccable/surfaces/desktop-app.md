## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Lattice is a photographic proofing bench for hiding and recovering local data, not a hacker console or generic file form. The carrier image stays visually dominant while the controls read as one calm processing sequence.

**OWN-WORLD:** Matte proof-paper surfaces use warm ivory, near-black ink, muted sage state marks, and a single brick-red action. Fine crop marks, measurement rules, and contact-proof captions provide structure; familiar desktop controls remain obvious and keyboard legible.

**STORY:** The user chooses a carrier image, supplies text or a file, sees the exact available capacity, chooses an output, and creates a new PNG. Recover mirrors that sequence and makes the safe non-execution boundary explicit.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A compact title and Hide / Recover switch sit above one wide image proof occupying roughly two-thirds of the height. Below it, payload, capacity, and output stations form a single horizontal bench ending in the primary action. At narrower widths the bench reflows into a vertical docket without shrinking the image into a token thumbnail.

**FORM:** Contact Sheet, grounded candidate six, assigned by direction seed `e5f4e669`; Wide proof composition approved. The signature interaction is the proof frame changing from an empty crop field to the selected image while its measurement notes and capacity resolve together.
